# Upload your classroom materials here

In GitHub, open this folder, choose **Add file → Upload files**, drag in your files, then **Commit changes**. Netlify rebuilds the website automatically after it is connected to this repository.

Supported: PowerPoint (.pptx), PDF with selectable text, Word (.docx), plain text (.txt), Markdown (.md). Unzip ZIP archives before uploading. Old .ppt files, scanned PDFs, pictures, audio and slide speaker notes are not read. PPTX extraction reads slide text in slide order; image-only content needs a text version.

Include the week in every filename, e.g. `Week 5 Monday Interview.pptx`. The chatbot uses all materials through `maxWeek` in the root `classroom-config.json`. Change that number as your class progresses. Documents without a week cause a build error so they cannot accidentally introduce advanced material.

Do not include this README as teaching content. Start by uploading your own slides; no invented sample curriculum is used. Without materials the chatbot refuses to start.

This repository is PUBLIC: uploaded files can be read by anyone. Upload only materials you may share publicly; no student names, submissions, grades or private data. Change repository visibility to private before uploading private teaching files.

The level limits are instructions to an AI and may occasionally be exceeded. Preview a conversation after changing your materials. Excessively large material sets cause a clear error rather than silently omitting content.
